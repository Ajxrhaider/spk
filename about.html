About Our Academy

Discover Skillpath

A place to learn.
A foundation to flourish.

From the first steps of childhood to the growing independence of secondary school, we nurture knowledge, character and practical skills.

Skillpath International Academy is a comprehensive school in Karu, FCT, Abuja, offering crèche, nursery, primary and secondary education. Every child brings unique abilities, interests and possibilities. Our purpose is to help them discover those strengths and develop the confidence to use them.

Our educational philosophy

Educating the whole child

Education should help children recognise their abilities, develop their strengths and learn to overcome difficulties. We support growth academically, morally, spiritually, socially and physically.

Our teachers encourage questions, discussion, practical exploration and problem-solving. Purposeful teaching, interactive feedback and structured support help learners make progress at their own stage of development.

We uphold discipline, high moral standards and reverence for God, encouraging pupils and students to respect themselves and others and use their abilities for good.
