Secondary School

From the earliest experiences of care to the growing independence of secondary school, our purpose remains the same: helping children learn, discover, achieve and contribute.

Secondary School

Our secondary section extends the academy’s commitment to academic achievement, character development and practical learning into the adolescent years.

We aim to help students become increasingly independent, disciplined and purposeful in their studies. Through academic work and broader school activities, we encourage critical thinking, communication, problem-solving and responsible decision-making.

Our educational philosophy recognises that secondary school is an important period for discovering interests and developing future ambitions. We therefore emphasise creativity, technological awareness, entrepreneurial thinking, leadership and service alongside academic learning.

We want our secondary school students to grow into young people who can express their ideas clearly, work constructively with others and approach further education and future responsibilities with confidence.
