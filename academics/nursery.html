Nursery School

From the earliest experiences of care to the growing independence of secondary school, our purpose remains the same: helping children learn, discover, achieve and contribute.

Nursery School

Our nursery section focuses on building a positive foundation for learning. We encourage curiosity, creativity, communication and friendly interaction, helping children develop confidence as they become familiar with school life.

At this stage, care and learning work together. We seek to make children feel welcomed, valued and encouraged, while helping them develop the habits of listening, participating, sharing and respecting others.
