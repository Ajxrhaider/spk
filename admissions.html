A New Chapter Begins at Skillpath

We welcome enquiries for crèche, nursery, primary and secondary school. Our admissions team will guide you through the appropriate entry class and the next steps.

Admission Process

  1. Choose the school section and class for your child.
  2. Complete the online application with a passport photograph and parent or guardian contact information.
  3. Keep your application number and print your photo slip.
  4. Receive exam and interview instructions from the admissions officer by email and phone.
  5. Check your admission status through the applicant portal.
  6. After admission, log in through the student portal and change your initial password.

What to Prepare

Provide the child’s name, date of birth, passport photograph, intended class, previous school information and guardian contact details. The admissions officer will confirm any supporting documents and assessment requirements for your section.
